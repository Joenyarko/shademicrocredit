How It Works

Three straightforward steps from initial consultation to fund disbursement. No tedious bureaucracy.

1

Submit Request

Complete our simple online application form or walk directly into our Lartebiokorshie branch opposite Mohdlana Pre-School with your Ghana Card and business details.

2

Rapid Verification

Our experienced credit officers review your application and visit your trade premises within 24 to 48 hours to assess cashflow and recommend an adaptable repayment plan.

3

Direct Disbursement

Once approved, funds are disbursed straight to your MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AT Money, or bank account so you can restock or invest without delay.
